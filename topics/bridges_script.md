# What If Every Bridge Vanished? (~45s Short)

1. HOOK: You wake up. Every bridge on Earth is gone.
2. The US alone has about 624,000 bridges. All of them, gone overnight.
3. New York City loses 789 bridges and tunnels. Manhattan is an island again.
4. Trucks can't cross rivers. Grocery shelves start emptying in days.
5. Trains stop dead at every river. Freight piles up on the banks.
6. Hamburg, Germany, loses about 2,500 bridges. It turns into islands.
7. Ferries and boats become the most valuable thing in the world.
8. Rebuilding? Engineers would need decades.
9. CTA: Follow for more. What should vanish next?
